## What is right and worth keeping

- The hourly setpoint is one shared resolver (`zoneSetpointHours`) and one shared check (`checkSetpointHours`), used by both engines, the seam and the referee script. That is Rule 11 done properly.
- The analytic step test (`setbacks.test (a)`) is a genuine hand recursion, and Engine 2 matches Engine 1 to 1e-6 K with heating switched off outside the hours.
- Both EnergyPlus findings are measured to the kilowatt, kept beside the new reads (`ideal_loads_reported`), and limited to stepped setpoints. Earlier referees are untouched. That is the right way to change a referee.
- The underfloor steady state by hand (`ufh.test (a)`) is independent of the engine's bookkeeping, and the EnergyPlus referee for underfloor heating passes on every line, at 1 and 4 steps.
